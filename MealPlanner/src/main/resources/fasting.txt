Type of Intermittent Fasting	Description	Meals
16/8 Method	Fast for 16 hours and eat within an 8-hour window each day.	Varies within eating window
5:2 Diet	Eat normally for 5 days and restrict calories (500-600) on 2 non-consecutive days.	Varies on eating days, restricted on fasting days
Eat Stop Eat	Do a 24-hour fast once or twice a week.	One large meal during eating window
Alternate-Day Fasting	Fast every other day.	Varies on eating days, none on fasting days
The Warrior Diet	Eat small amounts of fruits and vegetables during the day, with one large meal at night.	Small amounts throughout the day, one large meal at night
12:12 Method	Fast for 12 hours and eat within a 12-hour window each day.	Varies within eating window
